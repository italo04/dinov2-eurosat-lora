Dissecting and Adapting DINOv2 for Land Cover Classification:
An Architectural Teardown & Parameter-Efficient Study on Sentinel-2 Imagery

Alfredo Jimenez  •  Jhonatan Davila  •  Italo Perez
Grupo de Investigación en Inteligencia Artificial (IA-PUCP)  |  Departamento de Ingeniería, Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú (PUCP)  |  italo.perez@pucp.edu.pe
01 MOTIVATION & RESEARCH PROBLEM

Foundation Model Paradigm: Large self-supervised Vision Transformers like Meta AI's DINOv2 acquire robust visual features from web-scale natural photography.

The Remote Sensing Domain Shift: Satellite imagery (Sentinel-2 EuroSAT) presents unique challenges: nadir perspectives, multi-scale spatial textures, absence of focal foreground objects, and subtle inter-class spectral boundaries.

Core Question: Can low-rank adaptation confined strictly to late self-attention layers adapt DINOv2 to satellite land cover with minimal parameter overhead and zero catastrophic forgetting?
Figure 1: EuroSAT Sentinel-2 RGB multi-spectral tiles across 10 land cover categories (27,000 images).
02 METHODOLOGY & ARCHITECTURAL TEARDOWN

Phase A — Teardown of ViT-S/14: 22,056,576 parameters, 12 transformer encoder blocks ($d=384, h=6, \text{MLP}=1536$). Forward cost: 12.25 GFLOPs. Tesla T4 latency: 8.89 ms (fp32) and 1.17 ms (fp16, 853.7 img/s).

Phase B — Nucleus Intervention (LoRA): Freeze blocks 0–10. Inject low-rank matrices into Query ($Q$) and Value ($V$) projections of blocks 11 and 12: $$W = W_0 + \frac{\alpha}{r}(B \cdot A), \quad r=8, \alpha=8, \quad B \in \mathbb{R}^{384 \times 8},\ A \in \mathbb{R}^{8 \times 384}$$ Only 32,266 trainable parameters (0.146% of backbone).

Representation Caching: Precomputing frozen representations through block 10 preserves strict equivalence and slashes training time from ~8 minutes to 61 seconds on full EuroSAT.

03 CONCLUSIONS & EPISTEMOLOGICAL HONESTY
  • Core Finding: LoRA on late self-attention heads is highly effective for satellite adaptation, beating frozen features with 97.14% Macro-F1 ($p = 0.002$) with only 0.146% trainable parameters.
  • Representation Caching: Enables exact mathematical acceleration with zero degradation and zero runtime latency penalty upon merging.
  • Epistemological Honesty: Random stratified partitioning (seed 42) evaluates intra-distribution fit but does not measure spatial autocorrelation across geographic scenes.
  • Phase C Roadmap: Geographic cross-validation on disjoint regional biomes (EuroSAT GeoTIFFs) and cross-sensor evaluation with Sentinel-1 SAR imagery.

05 EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS & DISCUSSION

Quantitative Benchmarks: LoRA reaches 97.14% Test Macro-F1 and 97.28% Accuracy on 4,050 test images, outperforming the Linear Probe Baseline (95.80% / 96.00%) with $p=0.002$.

Major Infrastructure & Hydrology Gains: Highway F1 jumps from 91.87% to 97.72% (+5.85%) and River rises from 93.80% to 97.51% (+3.71%), while high-performing categories (Forest 98.89%, SeaLake 99.44%) maintain peak fidelity.
